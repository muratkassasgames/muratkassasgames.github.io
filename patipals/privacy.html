Privacy Policy

Pati Pals · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pati Pals does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your pets, their names, your progress, coins, purchases you unlocked and your settings (sound, music, language, notifications) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos

Photos you take in the game are kept in the game's album on your device only. A photo leaves your device only if you choose to share it through your phone's share menu, after a grown-up check. We never receive your photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device itself (for example when your pet wakes up or gets hungry). No server sends them and no data leaves your device. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Microphone

It is used only for voice commands, only after you tap 🎤 (a grown-up check comes first), and it turns off by itself after a minute of quiet. Speech is turned into text by the phone's own speech service (Apple or Google). Pati Pals does not record or store sound and sends nothing to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your name, email address or payment details. A grown-up check appears before every purchase.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The game has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It works fully offline.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: patipals.destek@gmail.com

隐私政策

Pati Pals · 最后更新：2026年10月24日

Pati Pals 不收集任何个人数据，不会将其存储在任何服务器上，也不会与任何人分享。

保存在你设备上的内容

你的宠物、它们的名字、你的进度、金币、你已解锁的购买内容以及你的设置（音效、音乐、语言、通知）都只保存在你自己的设备上。它们绝不会发送给我们或任何其他人。删除应用就会删除这些数据。

照片

你在游戏中拍的照片只保存在你设备上的游戏相册里。只有当你选择通过手机的分享菜单分享照片，并通过家长验证后，照片才会离开你的设备。我们永远不会收到你的照片。

通知

如果你允许通知，游戏会直接在你的设备上安排提醒（例如你的宠物醒来或饿了的时候）。没有任何服务器发送这些通知，也没有任何数据离开你的设备。你可以在游戏设置或手机设置中关闭通知。

麦克风

仅用于语音指令，只在你点按 🎤 后开启（之前会先进行家长验证），安静一分钟后会自动关闭。语音由手机自带的语音服务（Apple 或 Google）转换成文字。Pati Pals 不会录制或保存声音，也不会向我们发送任何内容。

应用内购买

购买完全由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理。我们永远看不到、也不会保存你的姓名、电子邮件地址或付款信息。每次购买前都会先进行家长验证。

没有账号、广告或追踪

游戏没有登录、没有广告、没有数据分析，也没有第三方追踪。它可以完全离线使用。

儿童

我们不会在知情的情况下收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有所变更，新版本将在本页面发布，并注明新的日期。
